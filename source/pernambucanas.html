Pernambucanas

A sign-up flow proposed for the Pernambucanas app, from the first screen to an open account.

Screens

The problem

People gave up before the account existed.

People were leaving sign-up before the end. The app asked for a lot up front: RG number, issue date and issuing body, parents’ names, marital status, city of birth, full address.

All of it came before the person had an account, or any reason to keep going.

Before

The sign-up in the app at the time.

What I did

What changed in the flow.

Desk research and a benchmark of other sign-up flows came first. The flow wasn’t tested with users.

  1. FirstAsk for less

    A short pre-sign-up opens the account. Address and store come later.

  2. PaceOne question per screen

    With a step counter, and the person’s name once the app knows it.

  3. DocumentsA photo, not typing

    Pick a document, photograph it, check the result.

  4. ErrorsWhere you’re looking

    The hint under the field turns into the error, in place.

  5. HelpAlways a way out

    Don’t know your CEP, need help, choose a store yourself, skip.

The flow

A selection from the proposed flow, from the original Figma file.

Errors

The hint becomes the error.

Each field has a short hint underneath. When something is wrong, the same line says what to fix, in the same place. No pop-up covers the field.

Documents

Take a photo instead of typing.

The RG fields from the old flow became one photo. Tips come before the camera, and a review comes after: did it come out well? Confirm it or take another. The selfie works the same way.

Later

Address and store come after the account.

Delivery address and preferred store aren’t needed to open an account. They’re offered once the account exists, and the person can come back to them any time.

A way out

Help on every screen.

Don’t know your CEP? There’s a link for that. Rather not share your location? Choose a store yourself, or skip. “Need help?” sits right under the questions people get stuck on.

Home

One account, two homes.

The Pernambucanas card lives inside the digital account, so each one gets its own home. A button at the top switches between them, and the header color shows which one you’re in.

Below it, the layout is the same in both, so nothing has to be learned twice.
