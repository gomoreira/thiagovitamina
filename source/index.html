Allstate Trade-In

Allstate Protection Plans had a desktop trade-in draft that had sat on a shelf for years. I rebuilt it as a mobile-first flow: six kinds of devices, several retail partners, thirteen screens.

Every step asks one thing. The gift card arrives before the device is sent, so trust is handled by the flow itself, not by small print.

Client
Allstate Protection Plans
Year
2023
Role
Product designer, via Avenue Code

After launch. Became Allstate Protection Plans’ white-label trade-in platform, now live with Staples Canada.

Music Intelligence Report

SoundCloud’s yearly data report was designed as a zine. I turned it into a website without losing the photocopy attitude.

A home that maps all eight chapters, a menu to jump anywhere, pages paced for scrolling. I also guided the Webflow build, so the motion and spacing shipped as designed.

SoundCloud

SoundCloud for Artists

Creators needed a clearer path to grow and earn on SoundCloud. I designed an academy around three tracks: get started, get heard, get paid.

From first idea to an approved, navigable prototype in two weeks. When the brand changed midway, I rebuilt it on the new guide, close to what is live today.

Client

Year

Role

After launch. Live at artists.soundcloud.com.

SafraPay

Banco Safra’s entry into card machines started with no product and no brand language. I led design at Ogilvy and designed hands-on.

The sign-up portal, the machine screens and the system behind them, kept premium on purpose. Its fee calculator turned a price list into something shop owners could test for themselves.

Client
Banco Safra, at Ogilvy
Year
2018–2019
Role
Head of design

After launch. Other players in the market later adopted the same calculator.
