Visões do Luvemba

Visions of Luvemba
Photography and video installation
2023

About the series

Visions of Luvemba investigates the continuities and transformations of mask cultures between Central Africa and its diaspora in Brazil, setting up a dialogue between the Bate-bola groups – a cultural expression born in the suburbs of Rio de Janeiro – and the cosmologies of Central African peoples, particularly the BaKongo and the Chokwe.

Born and raised in the western suburbs of Rio, my connection to the Bate-bola universe comes from a personal and territorial relationship with it. Despite its aesthetic and symbolic power, this culture has long faced stigma and marginalization. Emerging in the 1930s, Bate-bolas absorb influences from different mask traditions and popular celebrations, such as the Folias de Reis, building a visual language of reinvention, excess, and the transformation of the body through costume.

The mask is the centre of this research. Across African and Afro-diasporic cultures, masking is tied to bodily transformation, to the invisible, and to collective memory – not merely an object, but a passage between times, territories and ancestries. I often say these images are born from a dream, where mask, dance and music open the way to intangible worlds.

Visões do Luvemba, video installation, Novas Frequências Festival, Oi Futuro, Rio de Janeiro, 2023
