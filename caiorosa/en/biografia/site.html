Caio Rosa

Biography

Caio Rosa is a visual artist, photographer and researcher. His practice moves between photography, sound, sculpture and archival material to look at memory, cultural transmission and the histories behind images.

Drawing on family archives, Rosa’s work engages with Afro-Brazilian cultural traditions between Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais. Through experimental photographic processes, musical instruments, sculpture and sound installations, he looks at how rhythm, water and material transformation can carry historical memory.

His work has been shown at institutions and exhibitions including Pinacoteca de São Paulo, Museu de Vassouras, Rencontres d’Arles, Saatchi Gallery, Fotografiska Stockholm and the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna. In 2026 he was selected for the 15th Rencontres de Bamako, African Biennale of Photography, in Mali. He received the Prix Photo Aliança Francesa in 2024 and was an artist in residence at the Cité internationale des arts, Paris.
