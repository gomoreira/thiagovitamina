Memórias Congadeiras

Caio Rosa and his father, the musician and ethnomusicologist Spirito Santo, revisit Spirito’s photographic archive from his field trips to the Congadas of Minas Gerais in the 1970s – a living testimony to the fight for the recognition of African heritage in Brazil.
