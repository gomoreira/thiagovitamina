About the series

“I was the East of a compass with no North.
For some, the other.
The surplus value of shining skin, as controversial as time.
For many, the black milk that nourishes, the ever-living garden.
Absolution of all light and colour.
The crossing, the encounter, the passage.”

For Sale Series consists of self-portraits and archival documents that carry phrases, poetry, physical traits, bodily expressions and cultural codes, questioning the historical stereotypes attached to the image of Black people. The series brings into the present questions that come from my own experience and my reflections on photographic practice, state violence, the art market, mental health, and the cultural and performative multiplicity of the Black body in the world.

The work received the Prix Photo Aliança Francesa 2024 and is now part of the contemporary photography collection of the Bibliothèque nationale de France (BnF).
